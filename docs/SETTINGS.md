# Change the look and sound

## Choose a design

1. Open **Settings → App appearance**.
2. Choose **Classic / Standard**, **Material 3 Expressive**, or **Liquid Glass**.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** independently.

Classic is the default and uses lighter neutral paper surfaces. Material can follow your selected board palette or use a custom accent. Liquid Glass has **Full**, **Balanced**, and **Minimal Effects** tiers. Reduced transparency and high contrast use opaque surfaces. Theme changes preserve the current game.

## Change the game scene

1. Open **Gameplay background**.
2. Keep **Minimal**, choose **Ambient Glyphs**, or select **Off**.
3. For Ambient, choose a preset and adjust basic controls. Expand advanced controls for density, scale, tint, blur, speed, and frame cap.
4. Use **Preview on the board** or reset to defaults.

Ambient is confined to the game scene. It never animates the library, Friends, profile, or settings. Reduced motion uses a static frame; hidden scenes pause. Adaptive limits are explained in the setting and can be turned off.

## Choose event sounds

1. Open **Gameplay sound packs** and choose a pack.
2. Preview **Move**, **Capture**, **Check**, or **Checkmate**. Use A/B comparison to hear two packs.
3. Adjust master, gameplay, notification, and Sherine banter levels. Disable event categories for quieter play.
4. Expand advanced controls for per-event overrides. Restore pack defaults to remove overrides.

All packs are brief original synthesized event cues. There is no background music. Mute and Silent prevent playback; previewing still respects master mute. [Sound provenance](SOUND-LEDGER.md)

Preferences save on the device. Appearance, background, and audio settings sync after sign-in when the updated backend is deployed. Coaching and banter preferences remain labeled as device preferences.
