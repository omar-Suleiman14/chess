# Play a game

## Play Sherine

1. Open the homepage. Move with tap–tap, drag, or the board's keyboard controls.
2. Use **New** to choose another game. **Flip board** changes the view without changing your side.
3. Use **Undo** to return to your previous decision. Climb and Timed games become permanently unrated after assistance.
4. Finish the game. Use **Play again**, **Review game**, or **Back to board** in the result dialog.

Full Power uses unrestricted Stockfish 19 Lite with the selected thinking time. The Climb uses versioned opponents. Timed uses a separate rating pool for each clock preset.

## Ask for a hint

1. On your turn, tap **Hint** beside the game controls.
2. If asked, confirm **Using a hint makes this game unrated.** Cancel to keep eligibility.
3. Read the short **Clue**, then choose **Next hint** for **Highlight**.
4. Choose **Next hint** again for **Show move**. You still make the move yourself.

Only a current, legal, sufficiently searched suggestion unlocks the later steps. Moving, undoing, restarting, or changing turns clears help. Hints are unavailable while Sherine thinks or the game is over. [Fair-play details](RATINGS.md)

## Play online

1. Open **Play online**, choose a clock, and tap **Play**.
2. You're paired with the next player searching for the same clock; colours are random. Tap **Cancel** to stop searching.

## Start positions and odds

In **New game**, pick a start position: Standard, Queens only, Knights only, Pawns only, Pawns & queens, Random back rank (Chess960-style, no castling), or Queen/Rook/Knight odds (Sherine starts without that piece). These are normal chess from a different setup, so hints, review and Time Machine all work. Any non-standard start is unrated.

## Play a friend

1. Open **Play a friend** from the menu. Create an invite and share its PIN or link.
2. Your friend opens the invite and joins. Each browser keeps its own seat token.
3. After the game, choose **Request rematch**. Your friend can accept or decline; you can cancel a pending request.
4. An accepted rematch opens a new game for both players with colors swapped. The old game stays saved in the room.

The request expires after the server's two-minute deadline. A closed result dialog does not cancel it. Live engine help is unavailable in human games.

## Find and challenge friends

1. Open **Friends** (the people icon at the top of any phone page, or the sidebar) or **Players**.
2. Type part of a username. Results show whether you're already friends; tap **Add friend**, **Accept**, or **Challenge**.
3. A challenge opens a waiting room for you and alerts your friend: a banner on any page, a badge on Friends and More, and the count in the tab title.
4. Turn on **Challenge notifications** on the Friends page to be alerted on this device even when Chess by Chaos is closed.

Every signed-in player gets a username automatically. New profiles are public, so their card appears on Players; switch to private in **Profile** to show your card only to friends. Usernames stay searchable so friends can add you.

## Talk to Sherine

Tap her sprite and send a short greeting. Choose English, Egyptian Arabic, Franco, or automatic language following in Settings. Her dialogue never chooses the chess moves. [Character rules](SHERINE.md)
